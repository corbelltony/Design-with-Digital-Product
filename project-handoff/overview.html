01 / WHY IT EXISTS

Useful first. Personality included.

Cheddar Hub is a personal working prototype, not a commercial smart-home platform.

PURPOSE

Bring the daily stuff together.

The dashboard gives the builder one place to check time and weather, use local timers and reminders, open familiar media services, and check basic system information. Cheddar’s animated states make the interface feel like a companion instead of a plain utilities screen.

Recorded fact: This is an installed Raspberry Pi dashboard. This HTML overview is separate from that device and never sends commands to it.
AUDIENCE

Designed around its builder.

The primary user is the person building and operating Cheddar Hub at home. It is also useful to classmates, instructors, or future maintainers who want to understand how the prototype developed.

Daily useLarge touchscreen shortcuts and quick information.
Project reviewReal build decisions, testing, and unfinished work.

04 / WHAT COMES NEXT

Finish the proof, not just the look.

The next improvements are focused on reliability and a repeatable experience on the actual Pi.

KNOWN BOUNDARIES / FACTS

Where the prototype stands

  • Voice scope: only registered commands, not unrestricted conversation.
  • Latency: voice can take time to answer; existing footage preserves the delay.
  • External services: media and Google syncing still rely on their respective online services.
  • Current evidence: the Nov 13 release and Nov 17 demonstration do not replace a fresh final test.
NEXT STEPS / PLANNED, NOT COMPLETE

What should be tested next

  • Verify the actual installed version, screen navigation, and startup condition.
  • Record a continuous touchscreen workflow and repeat identical voice requests in a comparable setup.
  • Measure response delays and document any missed wake phrases, retries, or freezes.
  • Keep the raw evidence, note the limitations, and only then decide what to improve.
These items are planned test work. This overview does not claim that they were completed.
